# Changelog

## 0.1.0
First release: the best pieces from the DrakeMods suite, on their own (no DrakeModsLibs needed).
- **Display Case** (6 slots) and **Small Display Case** (3 slots), from DrakesItemShop: glass and wood cases that show what's inside lying on jute. Fill them from the door side; the glass door slides open while you do. Costs configurable, wood by default.
- **Public doors and chests**, from LockSmith: an always-open copy of every buildable door and chest (vanilla and other mods) in the hammer's Public tab. Anyone can open them, wards or not.
- **Paper (wall)** and **Paper (flat)**, from DrakesRenameit: blank sheets you write on like a sign.
- Each feature has a server-synced on/off switch that applies live; placed pieces always stay.
- Installed alongside DrakesItemShop or LockSmith, Essentials lets them provide their own version (same piece names, so worlds move between them).
